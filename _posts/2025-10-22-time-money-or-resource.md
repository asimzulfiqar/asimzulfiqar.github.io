On an unusual day,  
while wandering inside myself,  
I saw Time — sitting on the side.

In wonder I asked,  
"Why the long face?"  
"I am tired of my names," it said.

"What? What names?"  
"Money, resource, thief — and what not?  
People think what I am not,  
and abuse me with verbs."

"Verbs like invest, spend, waste —  
which I do not understand."

"Then who are you? Tell us."  
"I am me — I have my own identity.

I cannot be spent,  
I cannot be wasted,  
I cannot be invested like money.

I don't have an explanation in words,  
but you can consider me as your friend.

A friend with whom you sit  
without any expectation,  
a friend with whom you sip your tea.

If you will sit with me,  
I will stay with you.  
All the moments you lived  
could be brought back."

I will take you to unexplored worlds,  
worlds of wonder —  
where you might find yourself.

I nodded —  
"I see your point,  
but I need to go now.  
I have another meeting scheduled at four.

I'll meet you some other day,  
maybe when I will have time."

---

**— Asim Zulfiqar**  
(Inspired from the book [Metaphors we live by - Book by George Lakoff and Mark Johnson](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34459.Metaphors_We_Live_By))
