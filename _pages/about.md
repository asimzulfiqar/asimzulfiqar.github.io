Hi, I'm Asim—an IoT consultant, embedded systems engineer, and technical author based in Munich, Germany.

## What I Do

I specialize in building practical IoT solutions for real-world problems. Over the past several years, I've completed **230+ projects** ranging from industrial monitoring systems to agricultural sensors to e-commerce analytics platforms. My work focuses on making IoT accessible, reliable, and actually useful—not just buzzword-compliant.

Currently, I work as a **Scientific Programmer** on IoT research projects, particularly in agricultural applications involving sensor networks, data acquisition, and cloud-based visualization systems. I'm also an active **freelance consultant**, helping businesses design and implement custom IoT solutions.

## Background

I hold a **Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering** and a **Master's degree from the [Erasmus Mundus joint degree program in Photonics Integrated Circuits, Sensors and Networks](http://pixnet.santannapisa.it/)**—a joint program across universities in Italy, UK, and Japan. This international education gave me both technical depth and a global perspective on technology development.

I'm currently working toward a **PhD focused on IoT and embedded systems**, continuing to bridge the gap between academic research and practical implementation.

## Technical Expertise

My work spans the full IoT stack:

- **Hardware**: ESP32/ESP8266, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, custom PCB design
- **Protocols**: MQTT, HTTP/REST, WebSockets, Modbus, LoRaWAN
- **Software**: Python, C/C++, JavaScript, embedded firmware
- **Cloud**: AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, Azure IoT Hub, InfluxDB, Grafana
- **Specializations**: Industrial monitoring, agricultural IoT, sensor networks, data visualization

## Content Creation

Beyond client work, I create **technical content** to share what I've learned:

- **Blog**: Writing about IoT development, embedded systems, and software engineering
- **YouTube** ([@highvoltages](https://www.youtube.com/@highvoltages)): Video tutorials and project walkthroughs
- **Website** ([highvoltages.co](https://www.highvoltages.co)): Technical resources and project documentation

## Why IoT?

I'm fascinated by systems that connect the physical and digital worlds. There's something deeply satisfying about deploying a sensor in a field, seeing data flow through the cloud, and watching it solve a real problem—whether that's optimizing irrigation, monitoring industrial equipment, or tracking environmental conditions.

The best projects are the ones where technology disappears into the background and just works. That's what I aim to build.
