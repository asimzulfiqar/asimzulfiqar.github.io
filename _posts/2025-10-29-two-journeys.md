One should walk outside,
but also walk within. 

Outside, everything is separate; 
inside, everything is One. 

Outside, trees stay silent; 
inside, they whisper tales. 

Outside, foreign trips cost a fortune; 
inside, foreign trips are free — and far more real. 

The souvenirs you bring from outside may break or be lost, 
but the ones brought from inside will stay forever.

**— Asim Zulfiqar**
