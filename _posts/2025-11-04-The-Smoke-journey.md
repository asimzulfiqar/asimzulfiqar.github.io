How about,  
on a sunny day,  
on a beach,  
I smoked a cigarette full of weed.

As I smoked,  
the smoke rose up,  
drifted here and there,  
and slowly went inside me.

As I watched it dissolve,  
I became a particle of smoke  
and went inside myself.

As I descended,  
I saw colonies,  
colors,  
and fire.

Colonies of cells, viruses, germs, and bacteria.  
Colors — almost like rainbows and flowers —  
formed by the movement of neurons and blood.  
Fire — of war, anger, and jealousy.

As I went deeper into the bacterial colonies,  
I saw them talking.  
They were using "I, you, we, us, me" — just like we do.  
They were arguing,  
claiming land,  
defending their space.

Then I moved to the land of cells.  
They were busy fighting the viruses,  
falling one by one,  
but still fighting for their resources.

I peeked into the colony of viruses.  
They had their reason too.  
They just wanted to exist,  
to let their offspring grow.

Even the germs —  
were on strike.

And then I started to ascend,  
because I was smoke,  
and smoke never stays long.

I was released from my body  
and became me again.

As I became conscious,  
I wondered —  
if they all live inside me,  
and fight like we do,  
then maybe we too  
are living inside something bigger,  
laughing,  
arguing,  
and calling it life.

**— Asim Zulfiqar**
